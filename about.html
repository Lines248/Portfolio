About

I run operations and AI for small teams, and I build accessible tools to support that work.

At Dance/NYC I’m Operations Coordinator and I run the tech side of the org. I wrote our AI governance framework, set up our Claude workspace, keep our data clean, make updates to our website and build the trackers we use every day.

For the last two years I’ve run inline/access, my own studio, and I’m building a SaaS product for art galleries from the ground up. I build in React, Vue and plain JavaScript, with a full-stack Java background in API design, PostgreSQL and backend architecture. I like taking a design from Figma all the way to semantic, WCAG-compliant code that ships. I also build n8n workflows, including one that drafts security questionnaire answers for a person to review.

I came to engineering from art and fabrication at Cooper Union, where I learned to build with my hands and troubleshoot whatever broke. Living with a disability is a big part of why I test for access first. I’m a Deque Digital Accessibility scholarship recipient and working toward my IAAP certification.

Bringing AI into a nonprofit

Dance/NYC hired me to bring AI into the organization. Before we picked a tool, I wrote a framework covering policy, training, equity, funding and rollout, and presented it to our Executive Director. Then I set up our Claude workspace after reviewing its data and privacy terms, with a project for each department.

Separately, I built Can I Use AI for This? on my own, a small tool that turns an AI policy into accessible answers.
