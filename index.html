About

I’m a frontend product engineer working where AI meets real workflows. I build accessible interfaces and the systems that sit behind them.

At Dance/NYC I’m Operations Coordinator and I run the tech side of the org. That means leading our AI rollout on Claude, keeping our data clean, shipping UI updates and building the trackers and tools that let a small team scale.

For the last two years I’ve run inline/access, my own studio, and I’m building view, a platform for art galleries, from the ground up. I build in React, Vue and plain JavaScript, with a full-stack Java background in API design, PostgreSQL and backend architecture. I like taking a design from Figma all the way to semantic, WCAG-compliant code that ships.

I came to engineering from art and fabrication at Cooper Union, where I learned to build with my hands and troubleshoot whatever broke. Living with a disability is a big part of why I test for access first.
