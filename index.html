About

I’m a design engineer working where AI meets real workflows. I build accessible interfaces and the systems that sit behind them.

At Dance/NYC I’m Operations Coordinator and I run the tech side of the org. That means writing our AI governance framework, leading the rollout on Claude, keeping our data clean, shipping UI updates and building the trackers and tools that let a small team scale.

For the last two years I’ve run inline/access, my own studio, and I’m building a SaaS product for art galleries from the ground up. I build in React, Vue and plain JavaScript, with a full-stack Java background in API design, PostgreSQL and backend architecture. I like taking a design from Figma all the way to semantic, WCAG-compliant code that ships.

I came to engineering from art and fabrication at Cooper Union, where I learned to build with my hands and troubleshoot whatever broke. Living with a disability is a big part of why I test for access first. I’m a Deque Digital Accessibility scholarship recipient and working toward my IAAP certification.
