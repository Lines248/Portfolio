About

I work where AI meets real workflows. I build accessible tools and the systems that sit behind them.

At Dance/NYC I’m Operations Coordinator and I run the tech side of the org. That means writing our AI governance framework, leading the rollout on Claude, keeping our data clean, shipping UI updates and building the trackers and tools that let a small team scale.

Bringing AI into a nonprofit

Dance/NYC hired me to bring AI into the organization. Before we picked a tool, I wrote a framework covering policy, training, equity, funding and rollout, and presented it to our Executive Director. Then I set up our Claude workspace after reviewing its data and privacy terms, with a project for each department.

Separately, I built Can I Use AI for This? on my own, a small tool that turns an AI policy into accessible answers.

For the last two years I’ve run inline/access, my own studio, and I’m building a SaaS product for art galleries from the ground up. I build in React, Vue and plain JavaScript, with a full-stack Java background in API design, PostgreSQL and backend architecture. I like taking a design from Figma all the way to semantic, WCAG-compliant code that ships. On my own time I build n8n agent workflows that sort email and send form submissions into Notion.

I came to engineering from art and fabrication at Cooper Union, where I learned to build with my hands and troubleshoot whatever broke. Living with a disability is a big part of why I test for access first. I’m a Deque Digital Accessibility scholarship recipient and working toward my IAAP certification.
